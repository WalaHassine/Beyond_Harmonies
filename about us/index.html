About Us


Welcome to our innovative music visualization tool, a unique platform designed to transform your auditory experience into a rich, visual journey. Whether you’re a musician, a music enthusiast, or someone simply curious about the intricate world of sound, our tool offers an engaging and insightful way to explore and understand music through stunning visual representations.


Features


Interactive Audio and Visual Integration


Our tool allows you to use your microphone to sing or play sounds in real-time. You can also import your own audio or video files to see how they translate into visual art. This flexibility ensures that you can visualize a wide variety of sound sources, from live performances to recorded tracks.

Diverse Visualization Modes


Our tool offers a range of visualization modes designed to capture the essence and complexity of sound. Whether you’re interested in the intricate textures revealed by the Hilbert Scope, the color-coded frequency maps of the Spectrogram, or the dynamic paths and shapes of melodies, each mode provides a unique perspective on the music. These visualizations transform auditory signals into stunning visual art, allowing you to see the fine details and overall structure of your music in a new light.

Tailored for Different Music Types


- Basic Mode: Ideal for monophonic music, such as a single human voice, this mode simplifies the visualization to highlight individual notes and their progression.

- Piano Mode: Perfect for polyphonic music, such as piano recordings, this mode can handle multiple notes being played simultaneously. It’s also capable of visualizing live performances using a MIDI keyboard, offering a dynamic and interactive experience.

Technology Behind the Tool


Our visualizations are powered by advanced algorithms and machine learning models, ensuring accuracy and depth in the representations.

- Pitch Detection : The pitch detection functionality is built on a robust codebase, ensuring precise and responsive visual feedback for your audio inputs.

- Hilbert Scope : This visualization mode is based on sophisticated code that translates audio signals into visually intricate patterns.

- Piano Transcription : Developed using Onsets and Frames, a state-of-the-art machine learning model by the Magenta team at Google, this feature excels in transcribing piano music with high accuracy, capturing the nuances of polyphonic textures.

Our Vision


We aim to bridge the gap between auditory and visual experiences, providing a tool that not only entertains but also educates. By visualizing music, we hope to deepen your understanding and appreciation of sound, offering a new perspective on how we experience and interpret music.

Who Can Benefit?


Our tool is designed for a wide range of users:

- Musicians : Gain new insights into your performances and compositions by visualizing the intricacies of your music.
- Educators : Use visual aids to teach music theory and sound analysis more effectively.
- Students and Researchers : Explore the detailed structures of sound and music for academic purposes.
- Music Enthusiasts : Simply enjoy the beautiful and dynamic visualizations of your favorite tracks.
